The Project

F1 in Schools.
STEM in action.

F1 in Schools is a global STEM competition where students design, manufacture and race miniature Formula 1 cars. Our team, Aerial Racing, takes part as a school project — combining engineering, design and project management.

Design

CAD modelling and aerodynamic shaping of our scale model car, tested in a virtual wind tunnel.

Manufacture

CNC machining and 3D printing to bring the design to life with precision and care.

Race

Compressed-air powered racing on a 20-metre track, timed to the millisecond.
